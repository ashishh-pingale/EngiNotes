The EngiNotes Story

Engineering is tough. Between lab records, mid-sems, and internal assessments, students often struggle to find proper reference material. We started EngiNotes in 2026 as a simple repository for our own batch, but it quickly grew into a platform serving B.E. and B.Tech students across all branches.

Our goal is simple: To democratize education. Whether you are in a top-tier university or a local college, you deserve access to the best study materials.
