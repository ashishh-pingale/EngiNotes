ABOUT ENGINOTES

Making engineering less complicated.

EngiNotes was created with a simple belief: finding quality academic resources should not be one of the hardest parts of engineering.

01

THE STORY

It started with a simple problem.

Engineering is tough. Between lab records, mid-sems, projects and professional assessments, students already have enough to deal with.

Finding proper reference material often means searching through WhatsApp groups, scattered folders and old links.

We wanted to make that easier.

EngiNotes started in 2026 as a simple repository for our own batch. The idea was straightforward: put useful engineering resources in one organized place.

That simple idea became the foundation for a platform where students can find, share and discover knowledge.

“

Finding useful study material should be simple, accessible and free.

02

WHAT MATTERS TO US

What drives EngiNotes.

The platform is built around a few simple principles that shape how we want students to experience it.

01

Quality

We aim to make relevant, syllabus-oriented resources easier to discover and access.

02

Community

Students can share their knowledge and resources, helping create a stronger academic community.

03

Accessibility

Useful study material should be accessible without unnecessary barriers or complicated searching.

03

OUR MISSION

Democratizing engineering education.

Whether you're studying at a large university or a local college, access to useful study material should not depend on where you study.

Our goal is to build a place where engineering students can find the resources they need, contribute what they know and help one another move forward.
